TECHNICAL & GOVERNANCE AUDIT

Institutional Clarifications.

Answers to rigorous technical, regulatory, and architectural questions posed by Chief Risk Officers and General Counsels.

Does Veritas AI or any underlying foundation model train on our documents?
Categorically NO. We enforce a strict Zero-Data-Retention contractual SLA backed by liquidated damages. Your PDF documents and extracted vectors are loaded strictly into ephemeral, hardware-isolated RAM enclaves. The moment your user session concludes or the query completes, all in-memory structures are cryptographically overwritten. Under no circumstances are weights adjusted or fine-tuned on customer queries.
How do you eliminate hallucinations in complex legal and financial figures?
We employ a proprietary Dual-Attribution Verification Protocol. Rather than relying on generative free-association, our engine maps candidate answers against deterministic bounding-box OCR vectors. If any figure, percentage, or statutory reference cannot be verified with 100% mathematical correspondence to the raw PDF raster layer, the synthesis engine refuses to output it and flags the discrepancy to the analyst.
Can Veritas be deployed within private air-gapped data centers without public internet?
Yes. Our Sovereign Air-Gap tier is packaged as containerized Kubernetes binaries optimized for on-premises GPU clusters (NVIDIA H100/A100 or certified sovereign accelerator hardware). It requires zero network connectivity, zero external API pings, and zero telemetry reporting.
What document formats and scan qualities are supported?
Veritas processes standard digital PDFs, scanned historical microfiche, low-contrast photocopies, 90-degree rotated legal exhibits, and complex multi-page financial spread sheets. Our multi-model OCR pipeline preserves layout geometry, footnote associations, and mathematical formulas intact.
How does Veritas integrate with existing document repositories (SharePoint, iManage, OpenText)?
We provide enterprise connectors for Microsoft SharePoint GCC High, iManage Work, NetDocuments, OpenText, and private S3/Blob storage enclaves. RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) permissions are synchronized in real-time, ensuring users can only query documents they have explicit clearance to view.
